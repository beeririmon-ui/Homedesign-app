# Astra brief: a magazine-level front entrance for a warm-modern house (2026-11-15)

Answer in Hebrew. Be concrete and visual; no generic advice.

## Context
We are building a website for a home-design brand. The site is a virtual tour of a house. The first frame a visitor sees is the house's front door. A press on "to the living room" opens the door; the camera walks in, turns right and glides into the living room, where the products for sale appear with a light-line effect.

The owner rejected our first door frame (attached image 1) with these words: "the simplest, undesigned door... nothing to do with a design magazine... a simple, boring picture... not our direction."

The bar is our warm-modern magazine frame of the living room (attached image 2): low golden sun raking across textured plaster, an olive tree's shadow on the wall, lamps glowing, a sculptural niche, rich materials, layered depth, a view.

Fixed facts:
- The camera stands in a covered entrance court, about 3.45 m in front of the door, 24 mm lens, eye height 1.20 m, looking straight at the wall. The door sits on the left third; the inner corner of the court on the right third. The image is 16:9 and must read on a phone at about 390×220 px.
- The house style is warm-modern: lime plaster, smoked oak, walnut, travertine/limestone, bronze, linen, boucle; calm, warm, sculptural.
- Inside the door: a small hall, then a lined opening to the living room. The camera walk ends on the living room's existing tour image, which has soft, cool daylight from a big window (not golden sun).
- Objects drawn in the frame must be architecture, landscape, or things the brand could later sell (planters, outdoor lights, a bench, a doormat). Nothing generic that looks like a product we don't sell.

## What we want from you
1. **A short critique of image 1:** the five reasons it reads as boring.
2. **Three distinct directions for this frame**, each a magazine opening spread, clearly different from each other in a thumbnail. For each: a name, the idea in one line, the door (material, size, how it opens), the architecture in frame, planting and light fittings, the light and time of day, and how the light stays believable as the camera walks inside to a living room lit by soft cool daylight.
3. **For each direction, a prompt in English** (one paragraph) for an image model, at the framing above, photorealistic architectural photography.
4. **Your pick,** and one risk for an AI video model that walks through this door.

Output: Markdown, under 900 words.
