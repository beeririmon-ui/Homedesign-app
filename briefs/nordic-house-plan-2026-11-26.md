## What worked in warm-modern (the method to reuse)
- **One coherent hero render per room** in one light: plate → hero → layers extracted, with variants made as in-context edits. No separately rendered, pasted products. See FB13 (`docs/proposals/wm-fb13-living-wow-2026-11-25.md`, 3.2) and the README in `assets/renders/living-room/warm-modern/fb13-2026-11-25/`.
- **One hour for the whole house,** lamps on (FB16).
- **Studio stills for every wheel** (FB20).
- **A first-person, continuous arrival** and a guided route with one clip per leg (FB19, FB21).
- **Magazine-level art direction:** camera height and lens, the light, the hero moment (FB13 section 2).
