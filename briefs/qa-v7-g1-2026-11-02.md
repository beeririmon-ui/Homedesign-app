## What to measure
1. **The G1 criteria, every one you can measure, for each frame:**
   - the clay lock, **with the scale check**: `site/tools/transitions/pan_reproject.py register` and `check`, plus the edges of the fixed furniture (P6);
   - exposure and the light targets, against the Bible, marking BIB1 where it decides;
   - the forbidden list;
   - density;
   - the window;
   - product fidelity against each card's reference image and dimensions, as far as you can measure it (G1.1–G1.3).

   Where a criterion has no tool yet, say so; don't skip it silently.
2. **For the poses:**
   - does each pose agree with its master in the overlap, measured with `pan_reproject.py check` and each camera's yaw, u0, v0 from `docs/house-plan.json` and the round-4 `plan.json`?

3. **The products baked into each frame:** list them, and mark the ones whose stock is unverified (G8.6, `docs/render-products-check-2026-11-02.md`).
4. **A verdict per frame:**
   - **pass for the test**, **fail** (with the criterion and the number), or **cannot measure** (what is missing);
   - whether it may serve as a reference or an endpoint for the transitions.

master-designer is reviewing the same frames for design in parallel (`docs/render-v7-review-2026-11-02.md`). Don't wait for it, and don't judge design taste; measure.
