## The task
Build a prototype of the story layer as a test page, **not the live site**. Use the same route as `/test/` (`site/tools/make-test-page.ts`): for example `/test/story/`, generated by a new tool script.

1. **The opening.**
   - Use Nordic A as the cover: `assets/renders/editorial-phase-b-2026-11-06/nordic-A-ed4.png`.
   - It is full-bleed, in magazine typography and grid (studio rules א.6; ART decisions in `status/decisions.json`).
   - Add a short story line per room and style, taken from the review's §3.1 concept. Keep it in Hebrew, RTL.
2. **The story scroll.**
   - Nordic B (`nordic-B-fin.png`) as the second frame.
   - Then a "בסגנונות אחרים" strip with warm modern `warm-modern-A-ed4.png`, clearly marked "טיוטה".
   - Only frames QA called "final test" or "almost" go in (`qa/reports/2026-11-07-editorial-fin2-ed4-country.md` §4). The other four do not.
3. **Hotspots → "קנה את המראה".**
   - Add a quiet dot per visible starring product.
   - Tapping a dot opens a sheet: the product image from its card, name, price per the existing pricing code, and add-to-cart against the mock.
   - A "כל המוצרים בתמונה" list shows the frame's products. The product lists per frame are in the QA tables and the render records (`jobs.json`, `ed4.json`).
   - Put the hotspot points in a JSON per frame. Mark them **temporary, by eye**; qa-agent verifies them later (G-ED hotspot check).
4. **Entry to the tool:** a clear "עצב בעצמך" call that opens the existing room engine, its current Nordic living room.
5. **Phone first:**
   - 400 px and 1280 px;
   - reduced motion;
   - keyboard and screen reader for the hotspots and the sheet;
   - WCAG 2.1 AA.
6. **Images:**
   - Encode web sizes (AVIF/WebP) from the PNGs in the test-page pipeline, the same way the test page encodes its temporary frames.
   - Keep the first-load budget of the test page, or say what it costs.

## Rules

- Run the repo's checks: typecheck, lint, format:check, test, and the test-page screens.
