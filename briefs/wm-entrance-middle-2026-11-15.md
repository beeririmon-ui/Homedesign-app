## Why
Two answers from the owner on the same day (FB5 and FB6 in `docs/studio-rules-digest.md`):
- On the first door still (`assets/renders/hall/warm-modern/door-2026-11-14/d0.png`): simple, undesigned, boring, nothing to do with a design magazine. And "the building itself is really simple".
- On your concept images (`assets/renders/hall/warm-modern/concepts-2026-11-15/`, contact sheet `contact-sheet.jpg`): "עכשיו הדלת מפוארת מידי..משהו באמצע אולי?" He saw A ("חצר הזית") and B ("מראת המים") large, and C in the sheet.

**The target:** the entrance of a house people live in, at the level of the warm-modern magazine frame (`site/web/public/media/story/living-room/warm-modern/A/1280.webp`), but not a resort, a hotel or a gallery. The entrance of *that* living room's house.

**The manager's reading of what to keep and what to lose** (yours to refine):
- **Keep:** warm low sun and leaf shadow on textured plaster; natural materials (lime plaster, walnut, limestone or travertine); a living tree; a door with character; one or two warm points of light; a sense of depth.
- **Lose:** the scale and the statement. The giant pill niche and the wavy wall in A; the pool, the floating slabs and the 3 m pivot door in B; the drum in C. Nothing that reads as "look at me".
- **A useful test:** would a family in a good Israeli neighbourhood build this entrance for their own house? Would a furniture or lighting store owner see a home their customers could have?
