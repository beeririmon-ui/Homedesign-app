## Bottom line
- **58 new cards on 14 lines.** Blocking lines now with AliExpress candidates: house **22 key-tray (6)**, **24 exterior-wall-light (6)**, **27 gallery-stool (6)**, **15 entry table-lamp (3)**, **18 armchair-cushion (1)**; kids **ג0 roman-blind (5)**, which still blocks *sale* until a cordless/motor option and an EN 13120 report are confirmed.
- **AliExpress text search is down for our app today:** `aliexpress.ds.text.search` answers `NGSELECTION_SEARCH_ERROR` for every keyword and parameter set (11 tries; `ds.product.get`, `freight`, `image.searchV2` and `recommend.feed.get` work). The round ran on **`ds.image.searchV2`** (image search) seeded with photos of the closest known products (cached AE listings, and IKEA/Pooky photos already in our cards), then `product` → `freight` → `card`. Image search finds twins of the seed well and different shapes badly, so lines without a good seed photo came back empty, not because the market is empty.
- **Fill:** colours were measured from the in-room SKU photo (median-cut, studio background removed). Where the wall or window dominated the photo, the colour was read by eye instead (the notes say which). Dimensions come from the SKU label or title, and texture and visual weight from my look at the photos. Where the default SKU was wrong (a cup in a tray listing, white or green lamp bases, a blue "brown" pad), the SKU was changed to the one nearest the spec. **QA re-reads the colours under the scene light.**
- **Lighting from abroad is research only (ג.3):** no listing here declares CE or an Israeli plug. Kids lighting needs CE before sale (ד.7.3).
- Supplier photos were downloaded only to the session scratchpad. No supplier or store was contacted. Registry (`seen.json`) updated; rejected items are marked with the reason.
- Budget: about 250 API calls today (70 image searches, 66 product, 105 freight including SKU re-quotes, 11 failed text searches, 1 feed page), well under the daily cap.
