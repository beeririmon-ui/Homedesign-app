# FB10 sourcing round, 2026-11-18 (sourcing-agent): warm-modern bedroom and bathroom

## Result in one paragraph
Target was 6-10 candidates per line for 19 lines. This run delivered 22 new cards on 11 of the 19 lines (line 18 is covered by the candle cards of line 10); only line 6 reached 5-6, and 7 lines have no card (2, 3, 4, 5, 12, 13, 17). The run had only web search and single-page reads: no shell, no AE or CJ keys, no downloads. Home Center, Zara Home Israel and IKEA Israel pages could not be read, so the new cards come mostly from Fox Home (Israel, pages readable) and IKEA international pages (a proxy for the IKEA Israel range, not verified for Israel).

## Method and limits
- Official pages only, single-page reads. No scraping, no sign-ups, no contact, no keys, no phone numbers or business ids written.
- AE and CJ: not reachable (keys not set, and no shell). No AliExpress or CJ search was run for any line. The existing CJ and AE cards in the repo were not re-searched.
- Supplier images: not downloaded (no shell). Photo URLs are in the cards. Nothing was saved to the scratchpad `sourcing-fb10/` folder, it does not exist.
- `data/sources/seen.json` was not read or edited (new files only). Items opened this run, for registering later: Fox Home 0336802400, 0336582400, 0334069401, 0332899401, 0244886000, 0336002400, 0334540300; IKEA article numbers are in the card `sku` fields.
- Hebrew and English search returned summaries; where a page could not be read the card says so. Colours are estimates from the colour names, not measured from images. Style scores are sourcing estimates (the master-designer owns the real scores).
- `data/products/master/` did not exist in this checkout (the brief says 57 cards), so the folders were created fresh. The cards for lines 1, 6, 7, 8, 9 and 10 use new slot ids (`wall-shelf`, `table-lamp`, `rug`, `pouf`, `wall-mirror`, `nightstand-objects`) that are not in `data/slots/master.json` yet.
- Prices: Fox Home prices are the current sale prices (Black Friday), shown with list price in the card. IKEA prices are from the foreign page that could be read and are for scale only, not the Israeli price.
- Shipping to Israel, two options per card, in each card notes: Fox Home: cheapest = home delivery 19.90 ILS (free over 249), 6-8 business days (from the page); fastest = not published. IKEA: cheapest = IKEA Israel standard delivery if stocked; fastest = unknown. Neither was verified.
