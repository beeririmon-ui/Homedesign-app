Brief: `briefs/sourcing-hall-bench-console-2026-11-17.md`. Spec: walnut, matte oil; bench about 80 (70-100) x 40 x seat 45 with slab ends and a slab top, open between; console about 80 x 35 x 80 with a slab top on two elliptical drum or pedestal bases. master-designer picks; this file ranks.

## Bottom line
- **No candidate matches the form.** Nobody in Israel (or among the sources I could read) sells a walnut bench with two slab ends and a slab top at 80 cm, or a walnut console on two drum bases. The nearest finds are a solid-walnut thin-line bench (Tomik), a solid walnut entry bench (MESA, with a shelf) and a 120-160 solid bench in a walnut shade (Pony Style Siv). For the console, the walnut Israeli consoles are all drawer-and-iron-leg units; the form in the render would have to be made to order.
- **The practical F3 route is a made-to-order Israeli workshop** (Tomik, MESA, Baalot Melacha): all three say custom sizes on request, and one (Tomik) already sells solid American walnut. A quote for the exact render (80 x 40 x 45 bench; 80 x 35 x 80 console) needs a contact, which the brief forbids and rules ד.8 reserve for the owner. Plan B in rules ג.0 (no matching furniture: fixed, not sold) stays open.
- Prices are retail prices. A wholesale or partner price (needed for no-loss pricing) does not exist without contact, so scripts/economics/no_loss.py was not run (and no shell was available).
