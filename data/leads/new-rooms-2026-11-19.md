## Bottom line
- **16 cards on 10 of the 31 lines. The target was 6-10 per line: no line reached it** (best: dining rug, 4). **21 lines have no card.**
- **Reach:** web search and single-page reads only. No shell, so no downloads, no `seen.json` check or update, no `gaps.py`. **AE and CJ API keys are not set: no AliExpress or CJ search was run on any line.**
- **What worked:** IKEA Israel product and category pages (ikea.com/il/he) read well and give price, size, composition and photo URLs. Fox Home (foxhome.co.il) search and product pages read well. BUG (bug.co.il, toys) product pages read, with the first 100,000 characters only.
- **What did not:** Home Center (unreadable product blocks), Zara Home Israel (403, from earlier runs), search engines return mostly foreign IKEA pages. The IKEA category page for wall lamps resolved to a menu, not products.
- **What the market gave:** Israeli retail reachable here is IKEA, Fox Home and BUG. None carries the walnut, caramel-leather and espresso-ceramic items the documents ask for. The honest result is rugs and runners (good), and weak or off-palette matches for everything else.
- **Safety (kids):** no kids' card has an EN 71, OEKO-TEX or CE mark stated on its page. Recorded as "not stated" on each card. No cordless blind was searched.
- **Images:** not downloaded (no shell); the scratchpad folder `sourcing-new-rooms/` was not created. Photo URLs are in the cards. `usage_rights` is `unclear` on all cards. Colour hex values are estimates from colour names, not measured.
- **Style scores** are sourcing estimates; master-designer owns the real ones.
- **Not registered in `seen.json`.** Items opened, to register: IKEA Israel 30555282, 60561856, 00277395, 00623230 (polyester, rejected), 50515105, 60561875, 10561868, 60603026 (polyester, rejected), 40619066, 00385314, 50301897, 60178887, 00290778, 60598100; Fox Home 0335700300, 0335870300, 0321890300, 0313846000, 0320550200; BUG 12820, 11835, 12663, 628451387619.
