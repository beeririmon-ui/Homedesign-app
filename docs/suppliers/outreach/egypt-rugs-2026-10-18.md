## Kiliim (info@kiliim.com): sent 2026-10-18 from the owner's Gmail (thread 1a14d8e5d7aaa590)
The email asks five things:
1. **Consistency of handwoven repeats:** colour, pattern, size tolerance, the same design over time, and a photo of the actual piece before it ships.
2. **Sizes and lead times:** 140×200 / 170×220, 200–240×290–340, the hall runner 75×260, the kitchen runner 50×260 and the corridor runner 60×700, plus delivery time and carrier to Israel.
3. **Shipping and import:** the cost per rug, whether they can ship DDP, and what they know about Israeli duty and VAT. Also whether they provide a commercial invoice and a certificate of origin.
4. **Trade terms:** trade prices and minimum order, and which model they prefer (they ship each order to the customer, or we buy a few of one design in advance). Also whether we can order one sample first.
5. **Materials:** the fibre content and whether the dyes are natural.

## What we decide after the replies
- **Consistency good, delivery by DHL or DDP, and the price holds a 40–50% margin:** list the pieces. If production plus shipping takes more than about a month, the product page says so clearly ("זמן אספקה: X שבועות, עבודת יד בהזמנה").
- **Per-order shipping too expensive:** order a few pieces of one design in advance, and ship them to Israel together by DHL or consolidated.
- **No reply within 5 business days:** one short follow-up.
