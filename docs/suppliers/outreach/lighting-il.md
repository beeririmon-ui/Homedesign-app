## Message 2: only after a "yes" (Hebrew, a short outline)
Thanks, then the list of models from their catalog, with a link to each and the room or style it's for. Then the questions:
1. A trade or reseller price before VAT. Or another model that suits them: commission, or an order per sale.
2. Direct delivery to the customer: cost and time in days.
3. High-quality photos and permission to use them on the site. Can they shoot or send a sample for rendering?
4. **The Israeli standard** for each model: ת"י 20 approval, 220–240V, IP44 for the bath.
5. Returns and warranty.
6. Changes to order: shade colour or material, sizes.

## The rules (from the studio rules)
- Every message talks only about **that supplier's** goods.
- No commitment to an order, a minimum or a subscription in the first reply. Any payment becomes a question in the Studio.
- Replies are logged in `docs/suppliers/outreach/README.md`.
